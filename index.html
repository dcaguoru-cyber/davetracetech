Security and privacy

Trust must be designed into the product

These principles describe the intended architecture for a future application. They are not claims that a live banking platform has already been certified or deployed.

🔐
Multi-factor access

Combine account credentials with an additional verification factor and risk-based checks.

◉
Voice challenge privacy

Design voice verification so raw recordings are not retained longer than necessary and are not reused beyond the stated purpose.

⌖
Permission-based location

Use clear consent, granular controls and purpose limitation for any location information.

▦
Data minimisation

Collect only information that is necessary for fraud detection, response and agreed partner services.

↔
Protected integrations

Use authenticated, encrypted APIs and formal bank or fintech agreements rather than requesting online-banking credentials directly.

✓
Accountability and testing

Plan for threat modelling, penetration testing, audit trails, incident response and independent compliance review.

UK privacy approach: A future live product should be assessed against UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, including lawful basis, transparency, data-subject rights, privacy by design, retention controls and a Data Protection Impact Assessment where high-risk processing applies.

THE FOUNDER

Built to challenge reactive card-fraud prevention

David Aguoru is the founder of DaveTraceTech, a venture he has been shaping for over four years. A Cyber Security & Digital Forensics graduate and current MSc Network & Information Security student, David began developing the idea after experiencing the emotional and financial strain of card fraud firsthand.

What started as a simple concept called Card Location Tracker evolved through multiple prototypes, technical redesigns, and strategic refinements into DaveTraceTech—an ambitious, user-centric fraud-prevention solution. David’s mission is driven by a commitment to protect people from the stress, uncertainty, and financial loss caused by modern card fraud, transforming his personal experience into a powerful, long-term innovation journey.
